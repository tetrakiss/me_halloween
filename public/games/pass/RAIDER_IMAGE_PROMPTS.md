## rivet-spark

Use case: stylized-concept. Production asset atlas for existing Russian Fallout-inspired vault admission TV game. Exact 2 columns by 3 equal rows (six distinct rectangular cells), landscape3:2 overall, NO gutters and NO typography/UI. Consistent photorealistic monochrome muted OLIVE GREEN phosphor camera aesthetic, no other colors, no blood or gore. Row1: LEFT and RIGHT different raiders described below, chest-up front-facing portraits at same underground vault gate, large centered identifiable faces. Row2: LEFT and RIGHT separate formal ID photographs of SAME respective characters and SAME facial identity, plain aged olive wall, alternate lighting and slightly changed pose, not duplicate surveillance shots, keep identifying face accessories. RIGHT Mira Spark: lean woman28, short messy dark hair, narrow face, welding goggles on forehead, patched electrician jacket. Bags LEFT nuts bolts metal rivets pliers work gloves NO weapons; RIGHT wire coils batteries screwdriver cloth partly hiding compact pistol with identifiable grip and trigger guard visible but no highlighting.
