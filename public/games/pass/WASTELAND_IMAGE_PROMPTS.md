Режим: встроенный ImageGen. Итоговые файлы: `assets/waste-{dust,paper,rattle,crook,scrap}{,-id,-bag}.webp`. Для каждого — камера, целый рисованный пропуск и сумка. Рисованные пропуска показываются целиком вместо стандартных полей документа.

EXACT 2 columns x3 equal rows (six separate images), landscape3:2, no gutters, no UI overlay. Row1: live chest-up front-facing portraits of LEFT and RIGHT adult wasteland survivors described below, same vault gate background, distinct recognizable faces, torn patched clothes, no weapons and no gore. Row2: LEFT and RIGHT COMPLETE homemade identity cards on dirty rectangular scraps of cardboard. Each entire card includes a very childish hand-drawn face in a crude little portrait box at left (stick face, uneven eyes, crayon-like lines, resembling respective visitor accessories), and 4 rows of completely ILLEGIBLE shaky handwritten nonsense scribbles at right. Even heading is unreadable scribbles. NO readable words, NO real photo anywhere on cards, NO legible numbers, NO official printed typography or official seals. Badly drawn face and crooked handwritten lines must be obvious and large, comic but realistic graphite or charcoal on aged olive paper. Row3: top-down open bags specified below, NO weapons, sparse trash not valuable items. All three kinds of images fill respective cells, keep cards fully within cell. LEFT man50 nicknamed Dust: gaunt dusty face, tangled short beard, torn knitted cap and layered patched cloak. His homemade card drawn face has crude beard and cap. His bag is almost EMPTY, just crumpled dirty packaging and one bent bottle cap. RIGHT woman45 nicknamed Paper: tousled shoulder-length hair, large cracked eyeglasses, patched scarf, weathered kind face. Her card has absurd hand-drawn glasses and hair. Her bag contains torn food wrappers, flattened cardboard, broken plastic spoon and dirty string.
