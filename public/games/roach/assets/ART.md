# Radroach artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. Original PNGs retain
alpha transparency. No external image service is used at runtime.

Prompt set / production specifications:
- `table.png`: Fallout/Megatonna cinematic 3D materials, worn pale turquoise enamel
  tabletop, orthographic overhead camera exactly perpendicular to surface,
  empty playing area, warm upper-left light, rusty riveted rim; no UI or objects.
- `packet.png`: transparent isolated mustard/navy retro cookie packet, exact
  overhead camera, opened to the right with three scalloped golden biscuits;
  Russian text «ПЕЧЕНЬЕ» / «ДОВОЕННЫЙ ЗАПАС», atomic orbit print.
- `walk.png`: transparent 4 × 2 atlas, eight chronological walk-cycle frames;
  same chestnut/copper funny cockroach, lime eyes, head pointing right,
  orthographic overhead camera, fixed body registration, alternating tripod
  gait with six legs and waving antennae. Frame rate: 15 fps walking, 5 fps feeding.
- `laser.png`: transparent 4 × 2 atlas, eight chronological realistic laser impact
  frames, ivory ignition core, lime plasma halo, gold sparks, expanding ring,
  fading embers. Eight frames over 320 ms; code draws the white-core beam.
- `logo.png`: transparent playful 3D retro title «РАДТАРАКАНЫ» / «ПЕЧЕНЬЕ ПОД
  ЗАЩИТОЙ!», mustard enamel letters/navy bevels, funny cookie-holding roach,
  atomic star and lime laser accent. No background scene.
- `splash.png`: user's chosen second funny-roach concept, preserved unchanged:
  four cartoon copper cockroaches around a mustard/navy cookie packet on a
  turquoise vault cafeteria table, warm industrial kitchen light.

Music: three existing owner-supplied generated songs reused from
`../../bomb/assets/audio/radio-{1,2,3}.mp3`; attribution in that folder's CREDITS.md.
